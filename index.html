Most Aysha Siddika SumonaResearcher

I'm Aysha, a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at North South University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I work at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing and computer vision.

I started my B.Sc. in 2022 with a major in Robotics & AI and a minor in Theory of Computation, which gives me both practical and theoretical grounding. Courses like Differential Equations, Numerical Methods, and Pattern Recognition and Neural Network give my research a strong mathematical base.

My current work is aimed at CVPR 2027. I'm developing a unified foundation model for multi-class tracking, detection and segmentation, trained on more than 15 large-scale datasets, including Objects365, COCO and Cityscapes. Instead of a separate model for each task, I want one model that handles all three across very different visual domains.

In parallel, I'm reengineering the Kolmogorov–Arnold (KAN) theorem for large language model frameworks. The goal is to make these models more efficient and to remove algorithmic bias in the civil division sector of Bangladesh, so that language technology can serve public institutions fairly.

I've already published and submitted work across vision, language, audio and security. My conference papers include a comparison of Vision Transformers (PiT and CaFormer) for bone fracture detection, a study of vision-language models (BLIP, CLIP, ViLT and FLaVA) for movie genre prediction, and Screamio, a low-latency audio-only scream detection system built on Wav2vec 2.0 embeddings for public-safety alerts. My arXiv preprint enhances a BiGRU with a KAN block for legal document classification and summarization. Two journal papers are under review: a survey of scalable, secure blockchain-based storage with AI integration, and a lightweight deep learning approach to automated corrosion inspection of steel infrastructure.

I work in PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV and scikit-learn for AI and machine learning, and in Python, Java, C++ and PHP for programming. For robotics and embedded systems I use STM32CubeMX, Arduino and Thonny. I write my papers in LaTeX and manage my work with Git.
